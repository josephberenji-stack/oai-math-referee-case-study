**The submission's route (from a reading of the solution, not run).** Complex multiplication splits H¹ into
one-dimensional pieces, so every class is a combination of monomials, each a Hodge class exactly when its sign pattern
balances; a Hodge class is algebraic if each of its Hodge monomials is; monomials are joined by "pair switches" to
classes already known to be algebraic (divisors by Lefschetz (1,1), and degree two); the one new geometric step is the
"four-factor switch" on a product of four CM abelian varieties with v₀ + v₁ = v₂ + v₃ (where the Weil-type classes,
not generated by divisors, sit), realised by an explicit cycle from a surface in the fourfold product
(`algebraic_cycle_realizing_switch`, input `p54_input`). Everything else is supplied as classical theorems. Recorded for
the case study's reading of the submission only; it is not carried into any other work.
